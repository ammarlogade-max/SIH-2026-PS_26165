List of Gulf of Mexico OCS Region Safety Alert and Accident Investigation Reports Concerning Blowouts 
Recently, several blowouts have occurred on leases in the Gulf of Mexico, Outer Continental Shelf (OCS). So 
that a more proactive approach at preventing a recurrence of these accidents may be taken, copies are 
attached of previous Gulf of Mexico OCS Region Safety Alert concerning blowouts that have occurred in the 
Gulf of Mexico OCS. All OCS operators have an affirmative responsibility to conduct their operations in a safe 
manner; they should be familiar with these incidents, so that they may design and plan their work so as to avoid 
these problems. These Gulf of Mexico OCS Region Safety Alert are as follows:
