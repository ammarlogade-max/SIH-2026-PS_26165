CITATION  
SAFETY AWARD FOR EXCELLENCE  
CAMARILLO DISTRICT
PACIFIC OCS REGION  
HELMERICH & PAYNE, INC.  
PLATFORMS HARMONY and HERITAGE  
DRILLING CONTRACTOR
for EXXONMOBIL COMPANY  
    
   
This aw
ard is presented to Helmerich and Payne, Inc. (H&P) in recognition of its accident- 
free record and for the professional manner in which it conducted drilling operations on 
Platforms Harmony and Heritage for the period January 1 through December 31, 2003. 
During  the  rating  period,  Minerals  Management Service (MMS) personnel conducted 7 
drilling inspections at each facility with no incidents of noncompliance detected.  H&P, in 
conjunction with ExxonMobil Company, successfully utilized a combination of traditional 
methods and state-of-the-art extended reach drilling and completion technologies to access oil 
and gas reserves in the Hondo, Pescado, and Sacate Fields in the Santa Ynez Unit.  H&P 
consistently maintained an outstanding safety and environmental protection record while 
conducting  drilling  operations.  Throughout  the  year,  H&P  personnel  have  consistently 
demonstrated  a  keen  awareness,  knowledge,  and  application  of  pertinent  regulatory 
requirements.  Cooperation with MMS personnel was excellent. 
The Secretary of the Interior and the Director of Minerals Management Service join me in 
commending  H&P  for  its  part  in  the  development  of  offshore  mineral  resources  while 
maintaining a constant awareness for the safety of personnel, protection of the environment, 
and adherence to operating regulations. 
Peter L. Tweedt   
 Regional Manager  
Pacific OCS Region
