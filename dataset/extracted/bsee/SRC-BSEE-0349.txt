U.S. Department of the Interior 
Minerals Management Service
 
Gulf of Mexico OCS Region
 
Notice No. 170 
November 6, 1996 
Stabbing Board Fatality and Injury 
One fatality and one injury occurred aboard a submersible drilling rig while personnel were in the process of 
running casing. Two workers were on the stabbing board when the two bolts that attach the stabbing board to 
the vertical rails in the derrick were sheared. One worker fell approximately 40 feet to the rig floor, sustaining 
fatal injuries. The other worker remained suspended by his safety belt and retractable lifeline, which was 
attached to the derrick. A third worker on the rig floor sustained a head injury as a result of the falling stabbing 
board. 
It is recommended that stabbing boards be checked to ensure that they are properly bolted/anchored to the 
derrick. Additionally, personnel working in the derrick should always wear proper fall prevention equipment 
properly secured to a permanent part of the derrick.
