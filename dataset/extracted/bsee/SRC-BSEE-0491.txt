Safety Alert 42 - Hydraulic Pump Reservoir Ruptures Body A serviceman on an offshore platform recently received minor hand injuries when the fluid reservoir on a hydraulic pump ruptured. The pump was rated for high pressure, but the reservoir was capable of withstanding only low pressure.
