

A Safety Alert is a tool used by BSEE to inform the offshore oil and gas industry of the circumstances 
surrounding a potential safety issue. It also contains recommendations that could assist avoiding potential 
incidents on the Outer Continental Shelf.

BSEE!Safe Text Message Service Tops 6,000 Subscribers 
      
 
                     
The BSEE!Safe program, launched in May 2019, sends links to BSEE’s published Safety Alerts 
via text message to subscribers so they can receive actionable safety information without 
unnecessary delay. As of May 7, 2020, more than 6,000 people have subscribed to the service, 
which supplements BSEE’s long-standing practice of issuing Safety Alerts and Bulletins on its 
website, BSEE.gov and via email to operators.  
BSEE is the first safety regulator in the world to deliver critical safety information directly to 
offshore workers through text messaging and has issued 35 Safety Alerts and Bulletins via 91,100 
text messages since the launch of BSEE!Safe in May 2019. To view all of BSEE’s Safety Alerts, 
please click https://www.bsee.gov/guidance-and-regulations/guidance/safety-alerts-program
 
The BSEE!Safe texts contain the title of the Safety Alert along with a link to the published Safety 
Alert. Subscribers can opt out at any time. 
THEREFORE, BSEE Recommends:  
• Offshore workers subscribe to the BSEE!Safe Text Messaging Service at 
https://bsee.gov/bseesafe
 
• Share the BSEE!Safe links, alerts with workers who have not yet subscribed to 
BSEE!Safe.
