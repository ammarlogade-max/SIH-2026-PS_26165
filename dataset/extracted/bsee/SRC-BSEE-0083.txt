On March 21, 2020, President Biden stated, “This is a critical moment to accelerate our work to 
improve domestic cybersecurity and bolster our national resilience.” Accordingly, this Safety Alert 
is directed  to energy  operators  on  the Outer Continental  Shelf (OCS) to strengthen and 
systematize their  cybersecurity  defenses  and  regularly  monitor  the  guidance  issued  by the 
Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). 
Therefore, based on the potential for increased threats to U.S. infrastructure associated 
with the situation in Ukraine, BSEE urges all operators and contractors working on the U.S. 
OCS to immediately: 
·Review the information found on https://www.cisa.gov/shields-up and monitor it for
frequent updates.
·Ensure all known cyber vulnerabilities (https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-
vulnerabilities-catalog) are being addressed or monitored.
Safety Alert No. 434 
March 21, 2022 
SAFETY ALERT 

· Prepare the workforce for hyper-vigilance to detect abnormal network behavior and 
Industrial Control System (ICS) malfunction. 
· Test backup manual control equipment and processes and ensure they are workable in 
the event of any automated ICS compromise. 
· Report all suspicious and malicious cyber activity to CISA at report@cisa.gov or (888) 
282-0870 (note: over-reporting is recommended as it could mitigate widespread 
damage from an attack). 
· Inform BSEE via the Safety and Incident Investigation Division (SIID) Hotline at (877) 
440-0173 of any threats or compromises to OCS safety and environmental control 
systems associated with a cyber incident. In addition, notify the appropriate BSEE 
District office(s) if any offshore facilities are affected by the incident.
